PART 6 — CROSS-TEXT MULTIPLE MATCHING
- Four short texts (120–170 words each) by four writers, A–D, giving different views on the same academic or social issue.
- Four questions of the type: "Which writer shares writer A's view on …?", "Which writer has a different opinion from the others on …?", "Which writer expresses a similar view to writer C regarding …?"
- The texts must contain clear agreements and disagreements on three or four aspects so that each question has exactly one answer. A writer may be the answer more than once.
