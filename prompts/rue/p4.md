PART 4 — KEY WORD TRANSFORMATIONS
- Six independent items. Each has a first sentence, a KEY WORD in capitals, and a second sentence split into `before` and `after` with a gap between them.
- The gap must be filled with 3–6 words INCLUDING the key word, which cannot be changed in any way. Contractions count as two words.
- Each item tests two elements (e.g. passive + phrasal verb, inversion + fixed phrase, reported speech + noun phrase). `chunks` gives the two halves that earn one mark each, with acceptable variants of each half.
- `answers` lists every fully correct filler.
- The second sentence must keep the meaning of the first exactly.
- Mix structures across the set: inversion, passive reporting verbs, mixed conditionals, wish / if only, causative have/get, cleft forms, comparison, modal perfects, phrasal verbs, fixed expressions.
- You may refer playfully to the student's interests in one or two sentences.
