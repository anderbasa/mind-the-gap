PART 7 — GAPPED TEXT
- A coherent text (narrative, feature or memoir) of 500–650 words from which six paragraphs have been removed. Mark each gap as {1} … {6} on its own line between paragraphs; paragraphs are separated by blank lines.
- `paragraphs`: seven paragraphs A–G = the six removed ones in a shuffled order plus one distractor that fits the topic but nowhere in the text.
- Each answer must be fixed by textual links both before and after the gap: reference words (this, these, such), time sequence, contrast, lexical repetition and the logic of ideas.
- items[n].answer is the letter of the paragraph that fills gap n.
