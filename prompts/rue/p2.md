PART 2 — OPEN CLOZE
- A text of 200–280 words with 8 gaps marked {1} to {8}, in order. One word per gap.
- Test grammar and structural words: prepositions, articles, auxiliaries, pronouns, relatives, conjunctions and linkers, quantifiers, particles of phrasal verbs, parts of fixed phrases and comparisons.
- Avoid gaps that many different words could fill. If more than one word is genuinely correct, list ALL acceptable answers in `answers`.
- No contractions as answers (a contraction counts as two words).
