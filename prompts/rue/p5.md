PART 5 — MULTIPLE CHOICE
- An article or extract of 650–800 words with a clear point of view (a journalist, scientist or writer reflecting on something), in 6–8 paragraphs separated by a blank line.
- Six questions in text order testing detail, opinion and attitude, inference, purpose, implication and reference. Instead of line numbers, quote the words being asked about. The last question may focus on the text as a whole.
- Four options per question; distractors reuse words from the text but misrepresent it.
- `evidence` must be copied EXACTLY from the text (a phrase or sentence) so the student can find it.
