PART 3 — WORD FORMATION
- A text of 200–280 words with 8 gaps marked {1} to {8}, in order.
- For each gap give a stem word in CAPITALS (`base`); the answer is a different form of that word (prefix, suffix, internal change or compound), e.g. COMPARE → incomparable.
- Include at least two items that need a negative prefix and at least two that need more than one change (prefix + suffix, or suffix + plural).
- The answer must fit the gap grammatically (number, part of speech, tense). List every acceptable spelling (e.g. British and American variants) in `answers`.
