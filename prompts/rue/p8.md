PART 8 — MULTIPLE MATCHING
- An article in 4–6 sections (A, B, C…) of 140–200 words each, e.g. different people describing similar experiences.
- Ten statements to match with the sections. Each section is the answer at least once. Paraphrase: never copy the key words of the target section into the statement.
- Statements test specific information, opinion and attitude.
