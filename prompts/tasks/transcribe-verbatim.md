Transcribe this recording of an English learner (native Spanish speaker) VERBATIM.

- Keep everything they actually said: fillers (um, er, like, you know), false starts, repetitions and grammar mistakes. Do NOT correct anything.
- Mark pauses longer than about 2 seconds as [pause].
- If a word is unclear, write your best guess followed by [?].
- If they switch to Spanish, transcribe it as said and wrap it in [es: ...].
- Return only the transcript, nothing else.
