Rules:
- Spelling must be exactly correct (British or American spelling is fine; typos are not).
- Part 2: exactly one word that is grammatically and semantically correct in context. Maximum 1 mark.
- Part 3: one correctly formed word derived from the stem word that fits the gap (number, part of speech). Maximum 1 mark.
- Part 4: 3–6 words including the key word unchanged (contractions count as two words); the second sentence must mean the same as the first. 2 marks if fully correct, 1 if exactly one of the two tested elements is correct, 0 otherwise.
- Be strict: when in doubt, do not accept.
