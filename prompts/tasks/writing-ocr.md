This photo shows a handwritten answer to an English exam writing task, written by a learner.

Transcribe it EXACTLY as written:
- Keep every spelling, grammar and punctuation mistake. Do not correct or improve anything.
- Keep the paragraph breaks (use a blank line between paragraphs).
- Ignore crossed-out words.
- If a word cannot be read, write your best guess followed by [?]. Count how many words you marked this way.
- confidence: "high" if almost everything was clear, "medium" if several words were hard to read, "low" if the transcription is unreliable.
- note: one short sentence in Spanish about the handwriting (e.g. which words were difficult), so the student can check.
